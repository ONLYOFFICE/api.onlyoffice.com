## New features

- **Docs API**:
  - Added the [`document.options.watermark`](/docs/docs-api/usage-api/config/document/#optionswatermark) and [`document.options.watermark_on_draw`](/docs/docs-api/usage-api/config/document/#optionswatermark_on_draw) parameters: the watermark object, the `%user_name%` placeholder, opacity, watermarks in PDF and image downloads, and why a watermark is not access control.
  - Added the [Data retention](/docs/docs-api/get-started/how-it-works/data-retention) page: what ONLYOFFICE Docs keeps while documents are edited, where and for how long, who can access the cache, and how to delete it.
- **Common**:
  - Added an llms.txt link to the footer and named `/llms.txt` in the production robots.txt.
  - Added a megaphone icon to the announcement bar.
