## New features

- **Docs API**:
  - Added the [`document.options.watermark`](/docs/docs-api/usage-api/config/document/#optionswatermark) parameter: the watermark object, the `%user_name%` placeholder, opacity, watermarks in PDF and image downloads, and why a watermark is not access control.
- **Common**:
  - Added an llms.txt link to the footer and named `/llms.txt` in the production robots.txt.
  - Added a megaphone icon to the announcement bar.
